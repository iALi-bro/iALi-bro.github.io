iPayment · iOS

Track what you spend, in dollars and lira.

iPayment is a personal payment tracker for iPhone. Record what you paid, set a monthly credit, and see at a glance how much of it is left — in either currency.

What the app does

Every payment you record has a description, an amount, a currency, a payment method, and a date. iPayment groups them by month and totals them against the credit you set for that month, so the running question — how much is left — is answered on the first screen.

Two currencies

Enter amounts in US dollars or Lebanese pounds. Lira amounts are converted at the rate in effect when you enter them, so later rate changes never restate past payments.

Monthly budget

Set a credit for the month and the summary card flips between what you have spent and what remains.

Voice entry

Tap the microphone and say the item and amount — “bread 65000 lira” or “iphone 11 100$”. Works in English and Arabic.

Your own Drive

Optional backup to your personal Google Drive, in a private folder only this app can see. No account or server of ours is involved.

English and Arabic

The whole interface is translated, and follows your iPhone’s language and right-to-left layout.

Payment methods

Tag each payment as cash, Wish, or bank so you can tell where the money actually went.

How your data is handled

Your payments are stored on your iPhone. If you connect Google Drive, iPayment writes a single backup file into your Drive’s hidden application-data folder — a private area that only this app can read, and that no other app or person can browse.

There is no iPayment account, no server we run, and no analytics. Full detail is in the privacy policy.

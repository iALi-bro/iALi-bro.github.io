iPayment

Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 September 2026

iPayment is a personal payment tracker for iPhone. This policy explains exactly what the app accesses, what it stores, where it stores it, and what it never does. It covers the iPayment iOS app only.

The short version. Your payments live on your iPhone. If you choose to connect Google Drive, they are also backed up to a private folder inside your own Drive that only this app can see. There is no iPayment account, no server operated by us, no analytics, no advertising, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

1. Information the app stores on your device

iPayment records only what you enter:

  • Payment description, amount, currency (USD or LBP), payment method (cash, Wish, bank), and date.
  • The monthly credit amount you set, and its currency.
  • Your USD-to-LBP exchange rate and your voice-entry language preference.

This is held in the app’s local database on your device. We do not receive it and cannot see it.

2. Google account information

Connecting Google Drive is entirely optional; the app works fully without it. If you sign in, Google provides iPayment with your email address and basic profile information. Your email address is shown in the app’s Settings screen so you can tell which account is connected. It is not transmitted anywhere else and is not stored off your device.

iPayment never receives or handles your Google password. Sign-in happens through Google’s own interface.

3. Google Drive access

iPayment requests one Google Drive permission:

ScopeWhat it allowsWhy iPayment asks
drive.appdata Access only to the hidden application-data folder that Drive reserves for this app. It grants no access to any other file in your Drive. To back up your payment records so you can restore them on a new device.

Inside that folder the app maintains a single file, ipayment-sync.json, containing your payment records, your monthly credit amounts, and your exchange rate. Nothing else is written, and no other file in your Drive is read, modified, or listed.

The application-data folder is hidden by design: other apps cannot see it, and it does not appear when you browse Drive normally. The file is stored under your own Google account and remains your data.

Limited Use disclosure

iPayment’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google APIs is used solely to provide and improve the backup and restore feature described above. It is never used for advertising, never sold, never transferred to third parties, and never read by humans.

4. Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only while you are actively dictating a payment — after you tap the microphone button, and until the entry is added or dismissed. It is never accessed in the background.

Speech is converted to text using Apple’s speech recognition. Depending on your device, language, and settings, Apple may process the audio on its servers; that processing is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. iPayment does not record, store, or transmit audio itself, and the resulting text is used only to fill in the payment you are creating.

5. What the app does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, profiling, or advertising identifiers.
  • No advertising, and no sale or sharing of personal information.
  • No servers operated by us — your data goes to your device and, optionally, your own Drive.
  • No access to contacts, photos, location, or any Drive file outside the app’s own folder.

6. Your choices and how to delete your data

  • Stop syncing: tap Sign Out in Settings. Your data stays on your device.
  • Revoke access entirely: remove iPayment at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the Drive backup: in Google Drive, open Settings → Manage apps, find iPayment, and choose Delete hidden app data.
  • Delete everything on the device: delete the app from your iPhone, which removes its local database.

Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf. If you have a question about any of this, contact us using the address below.

7. Children

iPayment is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date above. Material changes affecting how Google user data is handled will be reflected here before they take effect.

9. Contact
